Privacy Policy

Office Tower Defense — last updated 1 September 2026

Office Tower Defense does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your progress — coffee earned, upgrades bought, best wave reached, which formats you have survived, and your wave-mode preference — is written to your device's own storage. It never leaves the device, it is not sent anywhere, and the developer has no way to read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores to Apple's leaderboards and reports achievement progress. What is sent is the score itself and the achievement identifiers, nothing more. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings at any time. Declining it does not restrict any part of the game.

Updates

The game checks for content updates when it starts. That request carries the app version and the device platform so the right update can be selected, and nothing that identifies you. It is served by Expo, whose privacy policy covers it.

What the game never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
